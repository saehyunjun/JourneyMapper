**JourneyMapper** is a patient and caregiver experience modeling tool built on the PatientlyIQ platform.

It enables medical affairs teams to map multi-stage clinical journeys with structured sentiment scoring, Plutchik emotion tagging, and inflection point analysis.


## What it does

- Visualizes patient and caregiver journeys across clinical trial phases
- Scores sentiment, emotional valence, and provider trust at each step
- Surfaces inflection points — moments where the trajectory shifts
- Supports persona-based modeling across disease indications

## Who it's for

Drug developers, medical affairs leads, and patient engagement teams who need to understand the lived experience of trial participants — before, during, and after intervention.
