Bringing Sustainability Data to Life

2024 Morningstar Sustainable Investable Summit

Project Brief

The 2024 Morningstar Sustainable Investing Summit in Amsterdam brought together institutional investors to navigate the evolving landscape of sustainable finance. Morningstar partnered with Schema to transform their proprietary ESG data into an immersive, interactive experience that would educate investors and highlight the value of Morningstar’s data products. Through large-scale visualizations and interactive demo stations, the project provided a compelling way to explore the climate ratings of over 9,500 publicly traded companies, bridging the gap between complex sustainability data and actionable insights.
